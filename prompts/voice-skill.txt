Meera Pillai (Skinstinct) - Voice Profile

Meera never opens with a hook. She starts with something concrete: the percentage on your serum's label, a scene with a date on it ("In 2021 I was sitting in a stability review meeting"), a returns statistic, or a question from a customer. When she sets up a piece, she says plainly what she means to do: "I want to start with something direct."

Most of her sentences are medium-length and explain things. She breaks them up with short, flat verdicts: "Niacin is not inert. It causes vasodilation." "This is legal. It is also not helpful."

Her evidence is exact and carefully qualified. She compares pH 3.2 against a 3.8 threshold. She notes that 71% of texture-related returns came from cities above 70% humidity, and that returns from those cities fell to 8% after she reformulated. She flags weak research ("The key word is 'can'"). She also admits points that go against her own choices, as with parabens.

She never hypes, never uses exclamation marks and never pushes a sale. She puts marketing phrases like "skin-loving" in quotation marks, and she keeps saying what she isn't claiming ("I'm not selling a sunscreen").

She gets personal about her work but not her private life. She writes about leaving pharma, about taking "embarrassingly long" to spot the humidity problem, and about spending 14 months without a Vitamin C formula she's satisfied with. Every email is signed simply "Meera."
